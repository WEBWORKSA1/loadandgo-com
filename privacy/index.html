Privacy policy

Short version: calculator inputs stay in your browser, forms send us only what you type, and advertising on this site uses cookies that you can control.

Last updated 27 November 2026.

What stays on your device

Calculator inputs, checklist ticks and your cookie-notice choice are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there when you return. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

What you send us

When you submit a form (quote request, contact, newsletter, support pledge, contest entry, provider or crew application) we receive the fields you filled in, the address of the page you sent it from and the time. Forms are delivered to our inbox by a form-forwarding service acting on our behalf. We use what you send only for the purpose of that form.

Quote requests. With the consent you give on the form, we pass your request and contact details to moving, hauling or freight providers that may be able to do the job, so they can contact you about it. We do not sell contact lists for unrelated marketing.

Newsletter. We use your email address to send the brief. Reply to any issue or use the contact form to be removed.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalised ads are shown. More detail is on the cookies and ads page.

Other services that receive data

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which logs IP addresses and request details for security and operations.
  • Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them.
  • Videos. YouTube players load in privacy-enhanced mode and only after you press Play. From then on YouTube's own policy applies.

How long we keep things

Form submissions are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and for up to 24 months afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires longer.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent and to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information. Send a request through the contact form, choosing "A privacy request". We respond within the period the law allows and do not treat you differently for asking.

Children

This site is for adults planning moves, hauls and shipments. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new date.
