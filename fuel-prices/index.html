Where the numbers come from

The U.S. Energy Information Administration surveys retail outlets across the country every Monday and publishes average prices for regular gasoline and on-highway diesel. This page reads that release on a schedule and rebuilds itself, so the table shows the most recent survey week named beneath it.

Which region are you in?

The regions are the long-standing federal fuel districts. East Coast runs from Maine to Florida. Midwest covers the Great Lakes and Plains states with Kentucky, Tennessee and Oklahoma. Gulf Coast is Texas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, Arkansas and New Mexico. Rocky Mountain is Colorado, Utah, Wyoming, Montana and Idaho. West Coast takes in California, Oregon, Washington, Nevada, Arizona, Alaska and Hawaii, and usually posts the highest prices.

Using a regional average well

An average hides a wide spread. Stations beside the interstate, in city centres and at truck stops with amenities charge more than the supermarket forecourt a mile away. For a long haul, price the trip with the average of the regions you will cross, then check the result against your first fill-up and adjust.
