Advertising cookies

Ads on this site are served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners set and read cookies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results and, where permitted, personalise ads using your visits to this and other sites.

  • Control or turn off personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising from many participating companies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The calculators keep working.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent message from a Google-certified provider asks for your choice before personalised ads are used, and you can change that choice at any time from the message's link.

Video players come from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain and load only when you press Play. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
