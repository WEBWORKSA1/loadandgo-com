What this site is, and is not

LoadandGo.com is an independent reference for people who load things: households planning a move, pickup and trailer owners, and small carriers checking a load. The calculators use published rules and typical equipment figures, and each page says where its numbers come from.

We do not rent trucks, sell boxes, move furniture or broker freight. When you ask for quotes, we read the request and introduce you to providers. Advertising and reader support keep everything free. Read how we work.
