Keep the calculators free

If a calculator here saved you a second trip, an overloaded axle or a bad load, you can help keep it running for the next person.

Where support goes

  • OperationsHosting, data refreshes, testing calculators against real loads and keeping pages accurate as rules change.
  • New toolsPaying developers to build the calculators readers ask for.
  • PeoplePaying writers, video makers and working movers, drivers and dispatchers who review our guides.
  • ContestsPrizes for the monthly loading contest.
  • ReachPromotion and outreach so the tools find the people who need them.

How to give

Use a payment button above when one is shown. Otherwise send a pledge with the form and we will reply with a secure payment link from a recognised payment provider. We never ask for card details by email or through this form.

Support is a voluntary gift to an independent publisher. It is not a charitable donation, is not tax-deductible and buys no goods or services. Thank you.

Other ways to help

Share a calculator link with someone who is about to move. Report an error. Write or build with us. Or sponsor a tool if you run a business in this field.
