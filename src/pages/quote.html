What happens next

  1. A person reads your request, usually within two business days.
  2. We look for providers that cover your route and job type, and check that they hold the licences the job needs.
  3. We introduce you by email. You compare, ask questions and choose, or walk away. There is no charge to you either way.

Get a better quote

  • Say how many furnished rooms or how many pallets, not just "a house" or "some freight". The truck size and freight class calculators give you the figures providers ask for.
  • Mention stairs, lifts, long carries, narrow streets and anything over 300 lb.
  • Flexible dates lower prices. Mid-week and mid-month are the quiet times.

Plain facts about this service

LoadandGo.com is a publisher. We are not a moving company, freight broker or carrier, we do not set prices and we do not handle your goods or your payment. Any contract is between you and the provider you choose. Providers may pay us a referral or advertising fee, which is one of the ways the calculators stay free. For an interstate household move, look up the mover's USDOT number in the federal database before you sign; our guide to avoiding moving scams shows how.
