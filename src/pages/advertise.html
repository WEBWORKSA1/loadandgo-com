Advertise and sponsor

People use this site in the days before they rent a truck, buy straps, book movers or tender a load. Put your name beside the answer they came for.

What you can sponsor

  • A calculator. Your name and link on one tool and its result ticket, labelled as sponsorship. One sponsor per calculator.
  • The weekly haul brief. A sponsor line in the email and on the home page brief.
  • A contest prize. Supply the monthly prize and be named as its sponsor.
  • The fuel price page. A natural fit for fuel cards, fleet services and truck stops.

Ground rules

Sponsorship is always labelled. Sponsors do not change calculator results, guide content or which providers we introduce to readers. We do not accept advertising for unlicensed movers or brokers.
