How to get a result you can trust

A calculator is only as good as the numbers typed into it. Three habits make the difference. Read ratings off your own equipment: the door-jamb sticker, the hitch stamp, the strap tag. Measure the outside of what you are loading at its widest points. And weigh instead of guessing whenever a public scale is nearby, especially for trailers.

Where a calculator uses a typical figure, such as the cubic feet inside a 16 ft box truck or the weight of a cubic yard of gravel, the page says so and lets you see or change it. Treat every result as a planning estimate and leave a margin.
