How it works

  1. Apply with your service type, coverage and licence numbers.
  2. We verify registration and look at complaint history before listing anyone.
  3. When a matching request arrives, we introduce you to the customer by email. Terms for introductions are agreed with you in writing first.

Who we work with

Local and long-distance movers, moving labor crews, junk removal and light hauling operators, LTL and truckload carriers, freight brokers, vehicle transporters and storage providers. Interstate household movers and brokers must hold active federal registration. We decline providers with unresolved patterns of complaints.
