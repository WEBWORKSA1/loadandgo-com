Join the crew

We are a small independent publisher that works with freelancers and people from the trade. If you know loads, or know how to explain or build things, we would like to hear from you.

Who we are looking for

  • Writers and editors who can explain a weight rating or a freight rule to someone meeting it for the first time.
  • Calculator developers comfortable with plain JavaScript and careful about edge cases.
  • Video creators who can film a loading or tie-down method clearly.
  • Industry reviewers: working movers, drivers, dispatchers and freight clerks who check our guides against the real job.
  • Partnership and sales people who know the moving, towing or freight markets.

How it works

Work is remote and by assignment. Paid assignments are offered as the budget from advertising, sponsorship and reader support allows, and the fee is agreed in writing before you start. We credit contributors by name unless they prefer otherwise.

We consider every applicant on skill and experience alone.
