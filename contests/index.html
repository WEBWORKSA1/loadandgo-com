The Best Load contest

Every month we pick the smartest load sent in by a reader: the tightest truck pack, the neatest trailer, the cleverest fix for an awkward item. Safe and legal loads only.

This month's prize

The prize is announced here each month.

How to enter

  1. Load something well. Take a photo or short video before you close the door.
  2. Send the form with a title, a few lines on how you did it, and a link to the photo or video.
  3. Entries close at the end of each calendar month. The winner is chosen in the first week of the next month and contacted by email.

How entries are judged

This is a contest of skill, judged by the editors on four things: safe weight placement, sound securement, good use of space and a clear explanation. Overloaded vehicles, unsecured cargo and anything hanging over a public road without flags score zero.

Rules

  • Free to enter. No purchase or payment improves your chances.
  • Open to adults aged 18 or over. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  • One entry per person per month. The entry must be your own load and your own photo or video.
  • By entering you allow LoadandGo.com to publish your entry with your first name. You keep the copyright.
  • Prizes are funded by reader support and sponsors and are as described on this page for the month in question. If no prize is listed for a month, the winner receives a published feature and no other prize. Prizes cannot be exchanged. Any taxes are the winner's responsibility.
  • The editors' decision is final. We may cancel or change the contest if it cannot run fairly.
