Official resources

Primary sources worth bookmarking. These are the pages we check our own work against.

Hiring a mover

  • Protect Your Move, from the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration. Search the registered mover database, read your rights for an interstate move, learn the red flags of moving fraud and file a complaint.

Securing cargo

  • 49 CFR 393.106: general requirements for securing cargo, including the aggregate working load limit.
  • 49 CFR 393.110: the minimum number of tie-downs by cargo length and weight.

Fuel prices

  • Gasoline and Diesel Fuel Update, from the U.S. Energy Information Administration. The weekly survey behind our fuel price page.

Freight classification

  • NMFC changes FAQ, from the National Motor Freight Traffic Association, on the density steps added in 2025.

Towing

  • Your vehicle owner's manual and the maker's towing guide for your model year. These hold the ratings that apply to your exact vehicle.
  • Towing capacity guide from hitch maker CURT, a clear walk through GVWR, GCWR, tongue weight and the lowest-rated-component rule.
  • Safe trailering videos from U-Haul, including how to load a trailer with more weight in the front half.
