How this works

The count uses a plain rule you can check by hand: a set number of boxes per furnished room (8, 12 or 16 depending on how much you own) plus five per person for clothes, shoes and personal things. The total is split 40% small, 35% medium and 25% large, because heavy items belong in small boxes and only light, bulky items should go in large ones.

Wardrobe boxes are counted at one and a half per person, and tape at one roll per fifteen boxes. Standard sizes are assumed: small 1.5 cubic feet, medium 3.0 and large 4.5. The last line shows the truck space those boxes will take, which you can carry over to the truck size calculator.

Questions people ask

Is it better to have too many boxes or too few?
Too many. Running out on packing night costs you time you do not have. Many sellers refund unused boxes, and neighbours take the rest.
Can I use free boxes from shops?
For light items, yes. Mixed sizes stack badly in a truck, though, and tired cardboard crushes at the bottom of a stack. Use new, same-size boxes for anything heavy or fragile.
