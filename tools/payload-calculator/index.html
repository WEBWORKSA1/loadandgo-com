How this works

Payload is everything the vehicle carries on its own wheels: people, cargo, accessories and the downward weight of any trailer on the hitch. The rating is GVWR minus curb weight. The yellow tire and loading sticker inside the driver door states it for your exact truck as it left the factory, in a sentence that begins "The combined weight of occupants and cargo should never exceed". That figure beats any brochure number, because options such as four-wheel drive and a larger cab eat into payload.

The calculator subtracts what you enter from the rating and shows what is left. Example: a 1,700 lb rating, two adults at 350 lb, a 100 lb bed cover and 600 lb of tongue weight leave 650 lb for the bed.

Questions people ask

Is payload the same as towing capacity?
No. Payload is carried, towing capacity is pulled. They interact because a trailer's tongue weight is carried by the truck and uses up payload.
Do passengers really count?
Yes. Four adults can use 700 lb or more of a half-ton pickup's payload before anything goes in the bed.
What happens if I go over?
Longer stopping distances, overheated brakes and tires, sagging rear suspension and light steering. It can also create liability after a crash.
