How this works

The budget adds six lines: truck rental (days times daily rate, plus miles times the mileage charge), fuel (miles divided by miles per gallon, times the pump price), paid help (helpers times hours times hourly rate), damage cover, supplies and travel extras.

The starting numbers are placeholders so the form is not empty. They are not a price list. Rental pricing varies by city, season and day of the week, so copy the figures from your own reservation. For a one-way rental quoted as a flat price with miles included, enter the flat price as the daily rate for one day and set the mileage charge to zero.

The fuel price is filled in from the latest U.S. Energy Information Administration weekly survey for the region you pick. Box trucks commonly return 6 to 12 miles per gallon loaded; the rental company lists the figure for each size.

Questions people ask

Is a DIY move always cheaper than hiring movers?
For a local move, usually. For a long one-way move the gap narrows once you add fuel, lodging, several rental days and time off work. Enter a mover's quote in the last field to see the difference for your move.
What costs do people forget?
Refuelling to the pickup level, damage cover, furniture pads and a hand truck, tolls for a commercial-height vehicle, and parking permits in dense cities.
How many helpers do I need?
Two people can load a one or two bedroom home in a few hours. For three bedrooms or more, a third pair of hands shortens the day more than it adds to the bill.
