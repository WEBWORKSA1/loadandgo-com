How this works

A box can face six ways. For each one the calculator counts how many whole boxes fit along the length, across the width and up the height of the space, multiplies the three, and keeps the best. Tick the upright option for liquids, plants or anything marked with arrows, and only the two upright layouts are tried.

The space count is then compared with the weight limit. Medium moving boxes at 30 lb each reach the payload of a 16 ft box truck at about 143 boxes, well before the cargo box is full. The gauge shows how much of the volume the final count uses.

Questions people ask

Why is the real number sometimes lower?
Wheel wells, a tapered roof, door hardware and bulging cartons all steal space. Take 5 to 10% off for anything other than rigid, uniform cartons.
Can mixing orientations fit more?
Sometimes a few, by turning the last row to fill a gap. Dedicated load-planning software does this; for planning a purchase or a rental, the single-orientation count is close.
