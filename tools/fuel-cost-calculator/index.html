How this works

Fuel burned is distance divided by miles per gallon, and cost is gallons times the price per gallon. The price field fills itself from the latest weekly retail survey by the U.S. Energy Information Administration for the fuel and region you choose, and this site refreshes that figure on a schedule. Type your own pump price over it at any time.

The number that matters most is loaded fuel economy. A pickup that returns 20 mpg empty may see 10 to 12 with a tall trailer behind it, and rental box trucks commonly return 6 to 12. If you add a tank size, the ticket also shows range per tank and the number of fill-ups to plan.

Questions people ask

Which region am I in?
East Coast runs from Maine to Florida. Midwest covers the Great Lakes and Plains states plus Kentucky, Tennessee and Oklahoma. Gulf Coast is Texas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, Arkansas and New Mexico. Rocky Mountain is Colorado, Utah, Wyoming, Montana and Idaho. West Coast is everything further west, with Alaska and Hawaii.
How do I measure loaded fuel economy?
Fill up, reset the trip meter, drive loaded, fill up again, and divide miles driven by gallons added.
