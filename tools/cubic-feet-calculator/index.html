How this works

Volume is length times width times height. In inches, divide the product by 1,728 to get cubic feet, because a cubic foot is 12 by 12 by 12 inches. Divide cubic feet by 27 for cubic yards, or multiply by 0.0283 for cubic metres.

For freight and moving, measure the outside of the packed item at its longest, widest and tallest points, including pallets, feet and handles. Space is sold as a box around the item, not the item itself. Enter a weight to see density in pounds per cubic foot, the figure that drives freight class.

Questions people ask

How many cubic feet is a standard pallet?
A 48 by 40 inch pallet loaded 48 inches high is 53.3 cubic feet. At 36 inches high it is 40.
How do I measure something that is not a box?
Imagine the smallest box it would fit inside and measure that. Carriers and movers do the same.
