How this works

Tick items as you finish them. Your progress is stored in this browser only, so it is still here when you come back on the same device, and nothing is sent to us. Use Print for a paper copy to tape inside a cupboard door.

The order follows how lead times work in practice: trucks, containers and movers book up first, paperwork and address changes take weeks to settle, and packing always takes longer than planned. If your move is closer than eight weeks, start at the top anyway and work down quickly.

Questions people ask

When should I book a truck or movers?
As early as you can fix the date. The end of each month and the summer season sell out first, and prices climb as the date gets close.
