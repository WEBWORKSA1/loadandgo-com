# Research log for LoadandGo.com

Compiled 26 November 2026. Every site below was opened and read directly (home page unless a path is shown). Notes record what each page actually showed.

## 1. Trademark check on the name

A search of public U.S. trademark records turned up live registrations built from the same words, in unrelated hands:

| Mark | Goods or services | Status seen |
|---|---|---|
| LOAD 'N GO | Rental of trucks | Registered 2016, maintained 2021 |
| LOAD 'N GO | Corrugated cardboard boxes; printing paper | Registered 2015 |
| LOAD 'N' GO | Utility bodies for trucks | Registered and renewed |
| LOAD AND GO | Portable household containers, funnels, scoops | Registered 2012 |
| LOAD & GO | Off-site treatment of petroleum catalysts | Registered 2016 |
| LOAD & GO | Class 39, transportation and storage | Dead (cancelled 2010) |
| LOAD & GO | Class 39 (Australia) | Registered |

**What this means for the build.** Truck rental and moving boxes are the two places where the name would collide with a live mark. The site therefore does not rent trucks, sell boxes or equipment, or offer transport under the name. It is positioned as an independent publisher of calculators and information, uses "load and go" descriptively, carries a non-affiliation notice in the footer of every page and a full notice at `/disclaimer/`. This is a risk-reduction design, not a legal opinion; a trademark attorney should review before any paid service is sold under the name.

## 3. What the study showed

1. **Calculators are bait, not product.** On rental, van-line and marketplace sites, a sizing or cost tool exists to start a booking. The explanation is thin and the tool stops at the lead form.
2. **The three audiences are served separately.** Moving sites ignore payload and towing. Towing content sits on hitch makers' sites. Freight maths (class, pallets, cost per mile) sits behind load-board and software sign-ups. None of the 42 pages read put all three under one roof.
3. **General calculator libraries have reach but no depth here.** They cover a few of these questions generically, without current fuel prices, securement rules or the 2025 freight-class change.
4. **Lead forms that convert share a shape:** job type first, two or three short steps, a consent checkbox, and a plain line about what happens next.
5. **Trust signals that can be copied honestly:** methodology, sources, review dates, a statement of what the site is not, and a visible route to report errors. Signals that cannot be invented (ratings, review counts, press logos) are left out.

## 4. Positioning chosen

**LoadandGo.com is the specialist calculator and reference site for anyone about to load something**, across household moves, pickup and trailer loads, and small-carrier freight. Revenue: display ads on high-intent pages, quote-request leads, labelled sponsorship per calculator, and reader support.
